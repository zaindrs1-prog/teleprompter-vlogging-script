Privacy Policy for TeleprompterPro

App name: TeleprompterPro
Developer: TeleprompterPro
Last updated: 02-10-2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the TeleprompterPro Android application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. It applies to the App and everything you do inside it.

In short: TeleprompterPro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Your scripts, recordings, and settings stay entirely on your own device. The App has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers, and it is not built to send information anywhere.

By installing and using TeleprompterPro, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Permissions We Use

TeleprompterPro requests only two Android permissions, and each one is used solely for the feature described below:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)
    Used to show the live camera preview and to record video with the App's built-in teleprompter. Video is recorded and saved locally on your device. It is never streamed, uploaded, or shared by the App.

  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)
    Used to record the audio track of your videos and to power the voice-sync scrolling feature that follows your speech as you read. Audio is processed and saved locally on your device. It is never sent off the device by the App.

The App does not request the Internet permission, location, contacts, storage, Bluetooth, or any other permission. Because the App does not have Internet access permission, it cannot send your data to a server even in principle.

You can review or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings under Settings > Apps > TeleprompterPro > Permissions. If you revoke them, the related features (video recording, audio recording, voice-sync scrolling) will not work, but the rest of the App will still function.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. None.

TeleprompterPro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, video, audio, scripts, or usage information. Specifically:

  • No personal information — no name, email address, phone number, or account details are collected. There is no login or account system.
  • No video or audio — recordings are saved only to your device's local storage and remain under your control. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No scripts or settings — the scripts you write and the settings you choose are stored only on your device.
  • No analytics or tracking — there is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • No device identifiers — no advertising ID or other device identifier is read or stored.

All processing — recording, saving, trimming, teleprompter scrolling, and voice sync — happens locally on your device. When you uninstall the App, any data the App stored on your device is removed with it (recordings saved to your device's gallery or shared storage remain there until you delete them yourself).

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with anyone.

There is nothing to share: no data ever leaves your device. The App has no servers, no cloud backend, and no third-party services embedded in it, so no data is disclosed to the developer or to any third party — whether for advertising, analytics, marketing, or any other purpose.

If you choose to share a recording yourself (for example, by exporting it to another app or uploading it to a platform), that action is entirely your own choice and is governed by the privacy policy of the service you share it with. TeleprompterPro is not involved in and has no access to that transfer.

Children's Privacy

TeleprompterPro is a general-purpose video recording and teleprompter tool. It is not directed at children and it does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Because the App collects no personal data at all, it does not collect or process personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below and we will address it promptly — though since the App has no way to transmit information to us, we do not expect this to occur.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if the App's features change or legal requirements change. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and post the new version at this same location.

Any changes take effect as soon as the updated policy is posted. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy. We will never change this policy in a way that makes our no-data-collection promise untrue for data collected before the change.
